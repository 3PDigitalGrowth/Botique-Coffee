With Boutique Coffee at Work in Melbourne, every rental is month-to-month, with one month's notice to exit and free machine pickup.

If you manage an office, you may have been burned before: a contract that was hard to exit, a helpdesk that never called back, or a machine that sat broken for a week. This page answers the questions Melbourne office managers ask us before they rent, without the sales gloss.

## Who is Boutique Coffee at Work, and why should we trust them?

### Who started the business, and why?

Chris started the business in 2008 because the gap between café quality outside the office and instant coffee inside never made sense to him. Melbourne workers walk past great coffee on the way in, then drink something worse at their desk. Closing that gap has been our only focus since. We call it café-quality, made practical.

Seventeen years, doing this exclusively and only in Melbourne. Founded in 2008, still Melbourne-only. That narrow focus is deliberate. All our experience sits in one place: Melbourne workplaces, their bench space, power and plumbing, and what a team actually needs from a machine. Our credentials page has the detail.

### Are you a big national supplier?

No, and that is deliberate. Chris's line is that he is not trying to be the biggest. Size tends to bring helpdesks, ticket systems and tiered support. We would rather stay small enough that one number reaches one person who already knows your machine, your site and your team.

## Is there a lock-in contract on an office coffee machine rental?

No. Every rental is month-to-month, with one month's notice to exit and free machine pickup. In 17 years, the no-lock-in policy has never cost us a client worth keeping. Clients who stay by choice are worth more to us than clients held in place by a contract.

Most equipment suppliers argue the opposite: lock-in protects revenue. That is true for the supplier. It does nothing for you if the service drops off in month four. Whatever provider you choose, read the fine print on minimum terms and automatic renewals. The ACCC publishes guidance on unfair contract terms in standard form contracts, and it is worth a read before you sign any equipment agreement.

### How do I end a rental?

Give one month's notice. We collect the machine from your site for free. That is the whole process. You do not need to find a buyer, arrange freight or argue over a termination clause. If the fit is not right, we would rather hear it early and either fix it or step aside.

### What is the difference between renting and owning a commercial coffee machine?

Owning means paying for the machine upfront, then carrying servicing, repairs and replacement yourself. Renting means the machine stays ours, and installation, training and ongoing service are part of the arrangement. Exact inclusions depend on the machine and plan, so read our article on commercial coffee machine service contracts and warranties before you decide.

### Will you try to sell us a bigger machine?

No. We recommend the machine that fits your team's actual size, even when that is the cheaper option. A 12-person team does not need a $15,000 Eversys. An 80-person team will break a home-grade Jura within a month. Matching the machine to the team is a point of integrity for us, not a sales tactic.

## Who do I call when the office coffee machine stops working?

You call one number and speak to one person: the person who looked after your setup. There are no call centres, no ticket systems and no escalation paths. Most issues can be talked through in two minutes when the person answering already knows your machine, your bench and your team.

### How fast is service?

The typical response time on any service call is 24 hours across our active client base. That is our own figure, not an industry benchmark. Same-day or next-day on-site response is only possible when there is no internal runaround to navigate first, and we have kept it that way on purpose.

### What does ongoing service involve?

After install, we settle into an ongoing rhythm of weekly or fortnightly service visits, topping up beans and consumables. The aim is simple: your team never goes without coffee. Scheduled servicing also catches small problems before they stop the machine at the worst possible moment, like a Monday morning.

That matters most in busy workplaces. Chris looks after a busy Melbourne workplace where the machine going down during peak office hours used to cause havoc. Regular scheduled servicing and direct personal contact for any issue changed that. The client's feedback is that reliable, regular service means the team always has coffee when they need it most.

The same applies in a busy retail environment, where coffee is served daily to staff and customers. Fresh beans, a fully maintained machine and one person managing supply and upkeep is what lets that business serve great coffee every day without interruption.

## Why No Lock-In Is Our Quality Control

Here is a position most suppliers will not take: a contract is not a sign of a good service. It is a substitute for one. When a client cannot leave, nobody inside the supplier feels the pressure to answer the phone quickly or turn up on time.

Chris sees the other side of this regularly. While attending to a client's site, he was approached by someone who worked at a previous client. That person had come specifically to ask for his card. He wanted to pass it to senior management, because the supplier they now had provided the worst coffee he had tasted, and he wanted Chris to meet them about new arrangements.

That is what the absence of lock-in looks like from the outside. Nobody at that office was held by a relationship. They were held by a contract, and they knew it. Our position is that we would rather be chosen every month than be stuck with every year.

It shapes how we work. One number, one person means there is nowhere to hide when something goes wrong. Month-to-month means the work has to be good every visit. And recommending the right-sized machine, not the biggest, means the first invoice is smaller but the relationship lasts longer. The 5+ year average is the result, not the goal.

The caveat: this model only works if you actually want a partner, not the cheapest possible box in the corner. If that is your priority, tell us on the first call, and we will be honest about whether we are the right fit.
